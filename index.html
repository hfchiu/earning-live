Live forward test

Two runs a day. The 15:45 ET signal is computed on intraday prices for names printing the next session, so it can be traded into the close. The official call is frozen on the closing price of the session before the print, matching the backtest definition, and is never changed. Each print is scored on its OHLC: close-to-close (the backtest measure), whether the favourable extreme touched the entry, and whether it reached a target equal to the spread. MFE and MAE are the best and worst intraday excursions from the prior close. Provisional calls for names further out are shown but do not count. Scorecard as of 2026-09-25.
